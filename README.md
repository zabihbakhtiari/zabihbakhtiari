<details>
<summary><b>What is holistic SEO and topical authority?</b></summary>
<br />
Instead of chasing single keywords, you cover a topic completely: a topical map of every service, question and location, linked together with clear structure and schema. Search engines and AI answer engines then treat the site as the authority on that topic.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is GEO and AEO?</b></summary>
<br />
Generative engine optimisation and answer engine optimisation: making sure a business is understood, trusted and cited by AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews, not just ranked in the blue links.
</details>
